### 👨‍💻 About Me

Passionate developer & researcher dedicated to bridging the gap between digital AI models and physical hardware systems.

- 🔭 **Focus Areas**: Machine Learning, Real-Time Computer Vision, Autonomous Robotics, and Edge AI.
- ⚡ **Core Strengths**: Deep Neural Networks (CNN, CRNN, ResNet, FaceNet), Embedded Systems (Raspberry Pi, Pico W), Pathfinding Algorithms (LSRB, Micromouse), and IoT integrations.
- 🌱 **Current Pursuits**: Self-Attention mechanisms in OCR, high-speed Odometry-driven robotics, and edge deployment optimization.
- 💬 **Ask me about**: Computer Vision, PyTorch/TensorFlow pipelines, C++ microcontrollers, or edge AI architectures!
